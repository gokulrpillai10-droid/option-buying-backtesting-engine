Verdict

6 lots is the better risk-adjusted fit for ₹5L

  • More return per unit of pain: return-to-drawdown 1.47 vs 1.25. Going to 9 lots adds ~50% more return but ~77% more drawdown.
  • Survivable drawdown: −19% (6 lots) is a recoverable dip; −34% (9 lots) is the zone where discipline usually breaks. Losing streak 6 vs 8 days.
  • Capital headroom: 6 lots peaks at 32% of the account in one position; 9 lots hits 48% — nearly half on a single deep-ITM trade.

Recommended pilot size: 6 lots. ~28%/yr at ~19% max drawdown on ₹5L is strong and holdable. Scale toward 9 only after living through a real drawdown and confirming live fills match the backtest.

Caveats — read before sizing

  • Lighter live: 2020–2025 figures use the old 75 lot; at today's 65 lot, live returns and drawdowns run ~13% lighter than shown (6 lots ≈ ~25%/yr, ~₹85k max DD).
  • Lumpy edge: 2025 ≈ half the profit; the strategy sits flat in high-vol regimes by design — expect long quiet stretches.
  • Low frequency: ~33–34 trades/yr — statistical power and recovery from drawdown both accrue slowly.
  • Validation basis: walk-forward + percentile-falsification + fill audit + volume floor all passed; engine reconciled 582/582 vs the reference strategy. Still a small-pilot candidate, not bet-the-account.
