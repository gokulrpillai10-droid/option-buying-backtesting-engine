Key Findings
Best entry window is 09:47–10:00. IV/RV₃₀ < 1.0 hits 25.9% of days at the 09:47 checkpoint. By 10:30 it collapses to 4.6%. If the opening 30-min RV is high (volatile open), options look cheap — that window closes fast.
IV alone is not selective. IV < 20% passes 73–75% of all bars across every time bucket — it barely changes intraday. The real signal is IV/RV₃₀ which discriminates sharply by time of day.
Median IV/RV₃₀ is 1.91× overall. NIFTY options consistently price in ~91% more vol than the 30-min window delivers. Options are structurally expensive vs intraday moves — "cheap" (ratio < 1.0) is genuinely rare (4.9% of bars).
